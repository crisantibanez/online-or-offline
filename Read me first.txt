ONLINE OR OFFLINE
A tiny menu bar app for the Mac that tells you whether your internet connection really works.

WHAT YOU GET
A dot at the top right of your screen, refreshed every 5 seconds:
  green  = internet works, with the response time in milliseconds
  yellow = it works but it is slow
  red    = offline (no answer within 3 seconds, or a login page is in the way)
Click the dot for details, a full speed test, and Quit.
Made for trains, hotels and flaky Wi-Fi: one glance before you press enter on something.

INSTALL (ONE TIME, ABOUT A MINUTE)
1. Move "Online or Offline" into your Applications folder (or anywhere you like).
2. Double-click it. Your Mac will say it "could not verify" the app and will not open it.
   Click Done. Do NOT click "Move to Trash".
3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the Security section.
   You will see a line saying "Online or Offline" was blocked. Click "Open Anyway",
   then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
4. The app starts and a green dot appears in your menu bar. From now on it opens normally.
   It never shows in the Dock while running: the dot is the sign it is on.
   To quit it, click the dot and choose Quit.
   To have it start when you log in, click the dot and choose "Launch at login".

WHY THESE STEPS ARE NECESSARY
Apple only lets an app open without questions when its maker has a paid Apple developer
account and has sent each version to Apple to be checked and stamped ("notarised").
This app is a free gift from a friend, not a product, so it is not notarised, and your Mac
treats it as unverified until you say you trust it. That is all those steps do.
There is nothing hidden in it: the whole app is a few hundred lines of code, published in
the open at https://github.com/crisantibanez/online-or-offline where anyone can read it
or build it themselves. It was entirely written by an AI (Claude) on Cristian's brief.

WHAT IT DOES ON YOUR NETWORK
Every 5 seconds it sends one tiny secure request to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1), the same kind of
request a web page makes, and measures how long it takes. A few kilobytes each time,
no account, no tracking, nothing stored.

TO REMOVE IT
Quit it from the dot, then drag "Online or Offline" to the bin. Nothing else is left behind.
